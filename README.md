# INSURANCE-EXCEL-
The Excel dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of insurance policy data using structured worksheets, pivot tables, and interactive charts. Policy distribution is analyzed across dimensions such as policy type, customer age groups, gender, s to understand portfolio composition. Time-based charts track policy issuance, renewals, and expirations benabling trend and seasonality analysis. Claim-related visuals show the count and status of claims (approved, rejected, pending) to evaluate claim performance and risk exposure. Premium and payment analysis charts examine premium growth, payment status, and payment methods to assess revenue flow . Slicers and filters allow users to interactively explore data by policy type, date, and customer attributes, delivering actionable insights for insurance operations and decision-making.
